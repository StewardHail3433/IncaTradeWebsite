So many people love the Empire and it's road system. For example, Pedro De Cieza De Léon had visited after the construction of the system 1450. Read about the thoughts from the Spanish conquistador, a Spanish and Portuguese colonizer, before the Spanish conquest.
In 1540 he publish his experience as a soldier, he found that the Inca Empire is highly organized with each province sending tributes of, “gold, silver, clothing, arms” to storehouses and estates that would consequently use these same resources to trade along its vast trade routes and roads across their empire. Furthermore, the Incas used quipus in order for everything to be “entered in the accounts” to keep track of the flow and movement of trade. he would say that throughout the high hanging bridges and the low valleys, the Inca Empire is the honored one. We want to thank the Cieza De Léon for the nice review (We do not apprecate the conquering of our land, like present day Peru, that he helped with in the future).
